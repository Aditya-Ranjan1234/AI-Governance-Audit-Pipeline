Keycloak adapter

Publishes identity events to governance.keycloak.v1. It owns GOV-001 through GOV-003: failed-login bursts, unapproved privileged role grants, and privileged login without MFA.

01

Failure isolation

A problem in one source adapter does not stop the other four producers or downstream consumers.

02

Independent scaling

Each adapter scales for its own event rate. The Kafka rule engine scales separately through consumer groups.

03

Least privilege

Each adapter’s IRSA role can write only to its own topic and the shared dead-letter topic.

04

Stable contracts

Services share the versioned event schema and Kafka topics, not databases or runtime state.

Risk comparison

Explainable baseline plus anomaly detection

Every finding keeps both scores. The deterministic baseline is easy to audit; the Isolation Forest can elevate unusual behavior that simple severity and frequency buckets might undervalue.

Baseline score

Severity and occurrence count are converted to a transparent 0–100 score.

severity × frequency band × 4
Example: severity 4 × band 3 × 4 = 48

Isolation Forest

The model learns the normal shape of recent findings after a warm-up period.

  • Severity and frequency
  • Rule and producer
  • Hour-of-day pattern
  • Baseline score
Example anomaly score: 71

Recommended risk

The pipeline retains both values and uses the larger score as the recommended operational priority.

max(48, 71) = 71
Baseline, model score, delta and model status remain auditable.
AWS deployment

Private, resilient and independently scalable

Terraform creates the network, EKS, three-broker MSK cluster, RDS PostgreSQL, ECR repositories and workload identities. Application services run in private subnets.

Governance VPC · three availability zonesTLS in transit · encryption at rest

Amazon EKS

Runs eight independent application services.

  • Five producer adapters
  • Three rule-engine replicas
  • Risk scorer and sink
  • HPAs and disruption controls

Amazon MSK

Durable contract between every service boundary.

  • Three brokers
  • Six partitions per topic
  • Replication factor 3
  • Minimum in-sync replicas 2

Amazon RDS

Stores audit evidence and Tableau-ready views.

  • Encrypted PostgreSQL
  • Multi-AZ option
  • Idempotent writes
  • Seven-day backups
